About

A scout is caught near a village storehouse and locked in a cell. No weapons and no combat: read the clues, solve three puzzles, slip past one patrolling guard and leave by the service gate. About 10–15 minutes, played through classic fixed cameras. The text of the game is in Ukrainian.

Your mission failed. The patrol took your gear, and at dawn you will face the village elder. The guards are ordinary people doing their duty, and the prison is plain stone, wood and iron. You win by noticing the clue, working out the mechanism and choosing your moment.

The graphics are made of simple shapes for now: the game was built to be played first and polished later. There is no magic and there are no creatures in this episode.

Features

  • Fixed cinematic cameras for every room, with smooth cuts at the doorways.
  • Three fair puzzles in one unbroken chain: a cell latch, a records cabinet with symbols, a gate with a two-part key and two levers.
  • One predictable guard with a visible loop, a pause at the window, and one hiding place. Being caught only sends you back a few steps; nothing is lost.
  • Inventory with a key-combining step, a journal with the clues you found, and hints that arrive after you have been stuck for a while.
  • Autosave, Continue, and a replay from scratch.
  • Alone, nothing leaves your PC and no account is needed. You can play with a friend (two prisoners in one prison); the network is used only for that game.
  • A sign-in screen like the one in Lords (Google or guest), which you can skip. Touch controls for phones. A Windows game that updates itself.

The episode

A. The cell

Scratches on the wall, a clay cup, a bent wire and a damaged latch.

B. The corridor

Torches, a guard on the round, a row of barrels in an alcove.

C. The storeroom

A ledger, a cabinet of symbols and your confiscated pouch.

D. The service gate

A workbench, a plaque and two levers beside a locked iron gate.

E. The courtyard

Night air and the way out.

System requirements

Windows 10 or newer, 64-bit, a graphics card that runs Direct3D 11. The game is light; it has not been measured on a range of machines, so no frame-rate promises are made.

Playing with others

Two prisoners can share one prison. Choose Play with others in the main menu: one player hosts, the other joins with the address it shows or an online code. The host's game decides the puzzles, items, doors and the guard; each of you has your own camera. If the guard sees either of you, you both go back to the corridor; whoever walks out through the gate ends the escape for both. Online games are not saved, so they never touch your own saved game.

Phones

Android and iOS builds are made from Unity Build Profiles. On a phone you walk with the thumb stick, use the Action button to take, use or hide, hold Run to go faster, and the bottom tabs open the inventory and the journal. They have not been tried on a device yet, and no phone build is published.

Updates

The Windows game updates itself from the newest GitHub release when it starts. You can turn this off in Profile.

Part of the world of Temnorid

The first time you press Play, a sign-in screen like the one in Lords offers Sign in with Google (on Windows, when it is set up on the device), Play as a guest or Play offline. You can sign in later from Profile, and a signed-in player starts with their Google name. The episode plays the same with or without an account, and offline.

Escape does not read or write anything in Lords: the capture of the scout is a local stand-in, and the escape changes nothing in Lords.
